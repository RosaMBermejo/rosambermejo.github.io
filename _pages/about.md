Hi! My name is Rosa, and I've recently received my PhD in Social and Health Psychology from Stony Brook University. As a graduate student in the [SPICE lab](https://you.stonybrook.edu/identitylab/), I examined how psychosocial experiences, such as feelings of belonging or discrimination, impact college students' well-being. As a research trainee in the [BIAS-NRT program](https://www.stonybrook.edu/commcms/bias-nrt/), I collaborated on interdisciplinary projects, such as examining post-conviction exoneration data and the intake decisions of innocence organizations. I've also obtained advanced certificates in [Quantitative Methods](https://catalog.stonybrook.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=12&poid=1753) and [Data and Computational Science](https://iacs.stonybrook.edu/opportunities/graduate-certificates/cdcs). 

Research Specialties
======
I specifically examine how vicarious racism, or indirect experiences of racism, impact personal mental health outcomes. For example, how do stories of racism from social media or from people you know impact your behaviors and mental health?
